McCausland College of Arts and Sciences

Discover more choices and more opportunities to build essential knowledge and skills for unlimited career paths. We prepare students to approach modern challenges with curiosity and lead with purpose in an ever-changing world. Through broad academics, groundbreaking research and real-world experiences, our students are equipped for success.

Prepared for the Future

With 38 majors, 65 minors and hands-on experiences, our rigorous and diverse programs prepare students to thrive in a global world. Pair disciplines, explore your passions and build a path to a meaningful career.

SVAD Centennial Exhibition

USC's School of Visual Art and Design marks its centennial with Generations: 100 Years of Art at USC, a McKissick Museum exhibition curated by students that spotlights faculty legacies, student impact, and the value of art education.

USC Launches Civic Center

The University of South Carolina is establishing a new academic center to foster a comprehensive understanding of the core ideals and values that have shaped American government and history.
