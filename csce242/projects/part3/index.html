About Us
The Foreign Collective is your one-stop shop for supporting our growing non-profit, dedicated to making a positive impact in local communities. Initially funded through our social media presence and exclusive merch sales, we bring together people who want to make a difference—whether by purchasing unique, meaningful products or by joining our mission. Every item and every follower helps us build a foundation for lasting change, empowering individuals and neighborhoods through direct support and creative collaboration.
Mission Statement
At The Foreign Collective, we honor the beauty of being foreign. Our mission is to remind the world that our differences connect us, shape us, and make us stronger together.

Fun Facts(This will be later turned into flashcards with JavaScript)

  • What does “foreign” means
    The word foreign comes from the Latin foris, meaning “outside” or “at the door.” It originally just meant “from outside the community or household,” and only later took on the sense of “from another country.”
  • The most is the most “foreign” animal you can think of
    The axolotl, a salamander from Mexico, is often called the most alien-looking animal on Earth. Unlike most amphibians, it stays in its larval stage for its entire life, keeping its feathery gills and a perpetual smile — so it looks like a cartoon creature but is very real.
  • What are some foreign foods we take for granted
    Tomatoes, potatoes, and chili peppers — staples of Italian, Irish, and Indian cuisines — all actually come from the Americas. Before Columbus’s voyages, Europe, Asia, and Africa had no tomatoes, potatoes, or chili peppers at all.
  • What is something foreign in space
    The Voyager 1 spacecraft, launched in 1977, is now over 15 billion miles away — officially in interstellar space. It’s carrying a “foreign introduction” of humanity: the Golden Record, with sounds and greetings in 55 languages, just in case aliens stumble upon it.
