Foreign Scholar
The Foreign Scholarship is awarded to the individual who best embodies the spirit of being "foreign." Whether through culture, perspective, or personal journey, this scholarship celebrates what makes you stand out and honors the beauty of differences that connect us.
WHO YOU ARE
The Who You Are Scholarship is all about authenticity. Applicants submit an essay about who they are, their story, and what makes them unique. The most powerful and genuine essay will be chosen for the award.
